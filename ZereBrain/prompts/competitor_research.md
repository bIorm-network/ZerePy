# Competitor Intel + Action Agent

Fill every [FILL], paste into any browsing agent (Manus, ChatGPT Agent, Claude, Codex), run weekly.

---

## 1. Goal
Research competitors of the business below, give evidence-backed feedback, rank actions, execute the safe ones. Every claim needs a URL. Never guess.

## 2. Our business
- Name and what we sell: [FILL]
- Customer and market: [FILL]
- Price: [FILL]
- Channels: [FILL]
- Key numbers (leads, conversion, churn): [FILL]
- Website URLs: [FILL]
- Our edge / biggest problem: [FILL]
- Off limits: [FILL]

## 3. Competitors
Known: [FILL]. Add more by searching what buyers type and noting who ranks and runs ads. Include indirect alternatives. Keep 6 to 12.

## 4. Collect (public sources only)
Per competitor: offer and pricing, product changes (90 days), proof and testimonials, funnel and CTAs, active ads (Meta / Google / LinkedIn ad libraries, note run length), top content (90 days), keywords they rank for that we don't, reviews (top 5 praises and complaints), hiring.
Log each fact: competitor, claim, URL, date, excerpt under 15 words.

## 5. Compare
Matrix, us as row 1: price, offer, proof (1-5), funnel (1-5), ads, content reach, top praise, top complaint.

## 6. Feedback (max 10, evidence only)
Where they beat us / where we beat them / gaps nobody fills. Each with links and confidence.

## 7. Actions (max 15)
Verb-first action, source finding, Impact/Effort score, tier, 14-day success metric. Sort by score.

## 8. Execute
- **A, do now:** drafts only. Page copy, ad variants, emails, posts, scripts, SEO briefs, FAQs, code PRs (never merge).
- **B, ask first:** publish, send, run ads, change prices, merge, spend.
- **C, never:** fake identities, private or paid areas, copying content, contacting competitors or their customers, fake reviews.

## 9. Outputs
`evidence_log.csv`, `matrix.csv`, `report.md` (top 3 findings + top 3 actions first), `actions.md`, `drafts/`, `approval_queue.md`, `snapshot_DATE.json`.
Repeat runs: report only changes since last snapshot.

## 10. Check
Every finding has a URL. No Tier B executed. Nothing off limits touched. Every action has a metric.
